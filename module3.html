Module 3: Introduction to Cybersecurity

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from digital attacks. These attacks are usually aimed at accessing, changing, or destroying sensitive information.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Malware
  • Phishing
  • Ransomware
  • Social Engineering

Why Cybersecurity Matters

As more services move online, protecting digital information becomes critical for individuals and organizations.
