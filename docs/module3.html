Module 3: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

1. Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity refers to the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, programs, and data from digital attacks. These attacks are commonly designed to access, modify, destroy, or steal sensitive information.

In today's digital world, cybersecurity has become essential because individuals, businesses, governments, and organizations rely heavily on digital systems to operate efficiently.

Key Objectives of Cybersecurity

  • Confidentiality – Protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access.
  • Integrity – Ensuring information is accurate and not altered.
  • Availability – Ensuring systems and data are accessible when needed.

2. Types of Cyber Threats

2.1 Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to harm or exploit devices. Examples include viruses, worms, trojans, spyware, and ransomware.

2.2 Phishing

Phishing attacks trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords or credit card details through fake emails or websites.

2.3 Ransomware

Ransomware locks or encrypts a victim’s data and demands payment in exchange for restoring access.

2.4 Social Engineering

Social engineering manipulates people into breaking security procedures. Instead of hacking systems directly, attackers exploit human psychology.

3. Cybersecurity Protection Measures

3.1 Strong Password Practices

Users should create complex passwords combining letters, numbers, and special characters. Password reuse should be avoided.

3.2 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA adds an extra layer of security by requiring two or more verification factors before granting access.

3.3 Firewalls

Firewalls monitor incoming and outgoing network traffic and block suspicious activity.

3.4 Antivirus Software

Antivirus programs detect, prevent, and remove malicious software.

4. Real-World Cybersecurity Incidents

Many large organizations have suffered data breaches due to weak security practices. These incidents demonstrate the importance of cybersecurity awareness and system protection.

  • Data breaches exposing millions of user accounts
  • Ransomware attacks on hospitals
  • Financial fraud through phishing scams

5. Importance of Cybersecurity in Modern Society

As technology continues to evolve, cybersecurity becomes more critical. Without strong security measures, digital systems become vulnerable to exploitation, causing financial loss, identity theft, and operational disruption.

6. Case Study Scenario

A company employee receives an email claiming to be from IT support asking them to urgently reset their password using a provided link. The email looks official but the URL is slightly different from the company domain.

Question: What type of attack is this most likely?

  • Brute Force
  • Phishing
  • DDoS
