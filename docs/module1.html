Module 1: Cybersecurity Foundations

1. What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from digital attacks. It ensures confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

🔐 The CIA Triad

  • Confidentiality – Data is accessible only to authorized users.
  • Integrity – Data cannot be altered without permission.
  • Availability – Systems and data remain accessible when needed.

2. Types of Cyber Threats

  • Malware (Viruses, Worms, Ransomware)
  • Phishing Attacks
  • Denial of Service (DoS)
  • Man-in-the-Middle (MITM)
  • Social Engineering

3. Practical Example

If a hacker sends a fake email pretending to be your bank and tricks you into entering your password, that is a Phishing Attack.

If ransomware encrypts your files and demands payment, it affects:

  • Confidentiality (data exposed)
  • Integrity (data altered)
  • Availability (data locked)

4. Certification Insight (Security+ / CEH)

Most entry-level cybersecurity certifications begin with:

  • Risk Management
  • Threat Identification
  • Basic Cryptography
  • Access Control

Understanding these foundations prepares you for certifications like:

  • CompTIA Security+
  • Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)
  • ISC² CC

5. Mini Lab (Thinking Exercise)

Scenario: You run a small company website. What security measures would you implement to protect it?

  • Use HTTPS (SSL certificate)
  • Strong password policies
  • Firewall protection
  • Regular backups
